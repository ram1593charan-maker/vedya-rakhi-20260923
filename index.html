Made by Hand

Three hours. One rakhi. Zero shortcuts.

My mother has been making rakhis the same way for 30 years — hand-stitched threads, hand-strung beads, and a small prayer tied in at the end for the brother who'll wear it.

We don't mass-produce. We don't outsource. Every piece in this collection was crafted at our kitchen table in Pune, between cups of chai and stories from the day.
